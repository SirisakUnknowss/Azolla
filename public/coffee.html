Buy me a coffee

Azolla is a one-person app — no ads, no accounts, no data collected. If it ever made a conversation of yours go somewhere good, a coffee helps keep it going.

Scan the PromptPay QR above with any Thai banking app and enter any amount you like. It pays for the Apple and Google developer accounts, the domain, and the coffee behind the next set of questions.

Not paying is completely fine. Every deck and every question is already free — a store rating or telling one friend about it helps just as much.
